The place

Rice, stone and square

A stone-walled dining room with an open kitchen and a terrace on Plaça del Prat de Sant Pere, opposite the monastery, right in Besalú’s medieval old town.

Rice cooked to order, seafood tapas and a family welcome. A meal with no rush.
