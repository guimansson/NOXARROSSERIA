Privacy & cookies

Data controller

The data controller is the owner stated in the legal notice.

What data we collect

This website has no forms or user registration. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects browsing data for statistical purposes (pages viewed, device type, approximate country). If you call or email us, we use your data (name, telephone number or email address) only to respond to your enquiry or manage your reservation, and we do not share it with anyone.

Legal basis and retention

To respond to an enquiry or manage a reservation, the legal basis is your own request, and we keep the data for as long as needed to respond to it. For analytics, the legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time; analytics data is retained for 2 months.

Hosting

The website is hosted by Netlify, Inc., which processes technical connection data (such as IP address and browser) for security and operation, as a data processor and with the safeguards required by the GDPR.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to the contact email. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).
